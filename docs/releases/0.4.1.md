# Cells 0.4.1 — absorption and recombination

Short visual absorption now covers captured cells, same-owner recombination and
consumed ejected mass as well as food. Cells shrink toward the visible absorber
for230ms and remain behind its body; their names travel/shrink with them. Food and
ejected particles retain the shorter170ms transition. Ejected-particle animation
starts at its last rendered position rather than its earlier launch position.
Reduced-motion preference removes travel immediately.

This is conservative client feedback based on disappearance, a nearby existing
visible cell and sufficient observed mass gain. Enemy captures additionally need
the previously visible eater to satisfy the1.18 mass ratio. Cells require95% of
their mass in observed growth. World-tick resets and changed player identities
clear eligibility. There is a32-event bound and the server's physics/protocol/
saves remain unchanged. Inference can miss ambiguous simultaneous events; it does
not establish an authoritative eat-event stream or award game mass.
