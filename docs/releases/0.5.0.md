# Cells 0.5.0 — Teams

Teams adds three persistent factions and a collective mass scoreboard over the
existing authoritative engine. New arenas carry30 programmed tactical bots,
balanced across color and profile. Humans are assigned by living-team population;
allies cannot consume one another. Ejected mass retains its faction color. A
small three-row scoreboard replaces the individual leaderboard only in this mode.
Classic, AI Arena and Expedição retain their rules and interfaces.

[Rules and persistence](../game-design/teams.md). New entry requires
`CELLS_TEAMS=1`, leaving deployment independently reversible. Existing team worlds
use the pre-existing binary team fields and persisted faction schema, including
safe rollback to0.4.1. No database migration or shared infrastructure change.

## Verified in the candidate

- Population test was RED with0bots, then GREEN with30 and fair faction/profile
  allocation. Save/reload retains every allegiance, profile and aggregate mass.
- Native ejected-mass metadata test was RED for faction0 vs owner1, then GREEN.
  Existing ally-protection and team-aware tactical tests remain enabled.
- Go race/integration/vet passed. Engine90.6%, expedition95.8%, protocol93.0%,
  bot90.2%, training90.0%, store87.5% statement coverage. No branch claim.
-20 TypeScript tests, lint/typecheck/build passed. Two actual two-client browser
  cases passed on desktop/mobile. Two rendering fixtures additionally verified
  proportions, own-team retention after death, exact ejected-mass color and axe.
  The initial fixture failed due to a wrong team-byte offset; fixing it to the
  established protocol contract made the fixture reflect the intended source.
- User-visible activation remains gated by the separately recorded final load
  benchmark and mainCI. Exact published SHA and public smoke/screenshots are
  attached to the semantic GitHub release after those operations complete.

Bots are heuristics; no new model training or promotion is claimed. Wider human
balance testing, physical Safari/Android profiling, WAN soak, off-host backups,
community features and further learning experiments remain follow-ups.
