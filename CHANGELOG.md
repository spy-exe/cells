# Changelog

## 0.4.1

- Captured cells, recombining pieces and consumed ejected mass ease toward their
  visible absorber; labels follow their body and remain below larger cells.
- Reset worlds and insufficient observed growth cannot manufacture absorption
  feedback; reduced-motion mode removes the travel.

## 0.4.0

- Enable the experimental solo Expedição on production after establishing the
  compatible0.3.1 checkpoint baseline: three timed sectors, increasing tactical
  waves, a Sentinela, two choices of persistent run mutations and new seeded runs.
- Classic and AI Arena retain their own rules; no cross-mode upgrades.

## 0.3.1

- Organic vector membranes and short food-absorption animations, with reduced motion.
- Centered adaptive names and ascending mass layering; outline-free viruses.
- Precise near-cursor movement,24 public Classic bots and32 AI Arena bots.
- Compiled, validated solo Expedição module behind a disabled production flag;
  compatible checkpoints establish a safe rollback baseline for its launch.

## 0.3.0

- Elastic vector-cell motion, growth feedback, optional synthesized sounds and
  accessible round summaries with server-owned statistics and keyboard respawn.
- Tactical food-goal commitment, danger/value overrides and complete persisted
  policy memory.

  digest-verified resume, held-out evaluation and deterministic command replays.
- [Validation and screenshots](docs/releases/0.3.0.md).
